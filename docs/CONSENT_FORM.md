# Voice recording and cloning consent form

Project: SwarKavach, voice-clone fraud call detection
Course: BCSE419L, Introduction to Natural Language Processing
Researcher: Bhavye Nijhawan (23BAI0100)

---

## What is being asked

You are being asked to (tick what applies):

- [ ] record yourself reading a set of short scripted phone dialogues
- [ ] allow a synthetic copy of your voice to be generated from that recording

## What the recordings are for

The project builds a system that detects fraudulent phone calls by checking
whether a voice is real and whether the conversation is a scam. To test it,
the project needs examples of both real and synthetic speech reading the same
scripts.

## What will happen to your voice

- Recordings are stored on the researcher's machine and in a private
  repository. They are not published, not uploaded to any public dataset, and
  not shared with third parties.
- If you consented to cloning, a voice model is generated from your recording
  using open-source text-to-speech software, and it is used only to read the
  fictional scripts in this project.
- The recordings, the voice model and every file derived from them are deleted
  at the end of the assessment, no later than **31 December 2026**.
- Neither the recordings nor the voice model are used to say anything you did
  not agree to read, and never to imitate you outside this project.

## What the scripts contain

Fictional dialogues. Fake bank names, fake case numbers, no real account
details, no real phone numbers. You will not be asked to say anything true
about yourself, and you will not be asked to read anything abusive.

## Your rights

- You can stop at any point during recording, without giving a reason.
- You can withdraw afterwards. Say so and everything derived from your voice
  is deleted within seven days. You do not need to explain why.
- You can ask to hear any synthetic audio made from your voice before it is
  used.
- Withdrawing costs you nothing.

## Risks

The honest risk is that a synthetic copy of your voice exists for the duration
of the project. It is kept on an encrypted machine, it is never published, and
it is deleted on the date above. If that is not acceptable to you, tick only
the recording box, or decline entirely. Recording without cloning is still
useful to the project.
